Guess the Element

Every element has its own properties and uses. In this quiz you get clues about a mystery element and must work out which one it is. The fewer clues you need, the more points you win.

  1. Read the first clue. It is worth 3 points.
  2. Need help? Tap Show next clue, but the points drop to 2, then 1.
  3. Choose your answer. You get one guess per question, and there are 10 questions.
