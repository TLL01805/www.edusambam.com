Photosynthesis Lab

Photosynthesis is how green plants make their own food. Leaves trap light energy and use it to turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose, releasing oxygen. In this lab you will build the equation, run a pondweed experiment, and find out what limits the rate.

Carbon dioxide + water — light, chlorophyll → glucose + oxygen
  1. Learn the six key ingredients and products.
  2. Build the photosynthesis equation.
  3. Run the pondweed experiment with three sliders.
  4. Find the limiting factor in three situations.
  5. Take a five-question quiz.
