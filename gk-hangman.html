General Knowledge Hangman

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time. Choose Basic, Intermediate or Advanced.

EDUSAMBAM Editorial Team|5 min play|General Knowledge · Basic, Intermediate, Advanced

Hangman is a classic word game that works well for vocabulary. Every word has a clue, and after each round you see the correct word.

Won 0Lost 0Streak 0Word 1 / 0
How to Play

Press a letter on the screen, or type it on your keyboard. A correct letter appears in the word. A wrong letter adds one part to the drawing. Find the whole word before the drawing is complete, then press “Next word”. Each word appears once before the list repeats.

Tips for Better Guesses

  • Start with common letters such as E, A, T and O.
  • Use the clue every time. It tells you what the word means.
  • Think about geography, science and history when you read the clue.
