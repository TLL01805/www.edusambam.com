Enzyme Activity

Enzymes are proteins that act as biological catalysts: they speed up chemical reactions in living things without being used up. Each enzyme works on one kind of molecule, called its substrate, which fits into a special shape on the enzyme called the active site.

Substrate + enzyme → enzyme–substrate complex → products + enzyme (unchanged)
