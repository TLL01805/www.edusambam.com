Fraction Pizza Builder

Build fractions you can see. Drag a topping onto the pizza — or just tap a slice — to top the right number of slices and match the target fraction.

EDUSAMBAM Editorial Team · 4 pizzas · Drag & Drop / Tap · Auto-Graded

Each pizza has a target fraction below it. Drag the topping onto slices to mark them — or simply tap a slice to top or untop it. Top exactly the right number of slices, then press "Check My Pizza". Any slices work, since every slice on a pizza is the same size.
