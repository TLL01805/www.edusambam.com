Section A — Multiple Choice

30 marks

15 questions · 2 marks each. Choose the best answer.

1.Bacteria are generally classified as:Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. Bacterial cells have no nucleus or other membrane-bound organelles, so they are prokaryotic.
2.Viruses are best described as:Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Viruses are not cells. They can reproduce only by using the machinery of a host cell.
3.A pathogen is:Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. Pathogens include some bacteria, viruses, fungi and protists. Many microorganisms are harmless.
4.Which statement about malaria is correct?Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. Malaria is caused by Plasmodium, a protist. Female Anopheles mosquitoes act as vectors.
5.Which of these diseases is caused by a virus?Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. Influenza is viral. Tuberculosis is bacterial, malaria is caused by a protist and athlete's foot by a fungus.
6.Which measure most directly reduces the spread of cholera?Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. Cholera is spread through water or food contaminated with the bacterium. Clean water and sanitation break that route.
7.Phagocytes help defend the body by:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. Phagocytes are white blood cells that engulf pathogens by phagocytosis and digest them.
8.The incubation period is the time between:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. During incubation the pathogen is multiplying but symptoms have not yet appeared.
9.A chromosome is best described as:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. Chromosomes are made of DNA wound around proteins, which packages long DNA molecules.
10.A gene is best understood as:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. A gene is a region of DNA that carries the instructions for a product, usually a protein or an RNA molecule.
11.DNA replication is described as semiconservative because:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Each new DNA molecule keeps one original strand and gains one newly made strand.
12.Meiosis is important in sexual reproduction because it:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. Meiosis halves the chromosome number to make gametes, and crossing over and independent assortment create new allele combinations.
13.A mutation is:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. Mutations can be harmful, neutral or occasionally beneficial, depending on where they occur and the environment.
14.Both parents are heterozygous carriers (Aa) of a recessive allele. What is the chance that a child inherits two recessive alleles (aa)?Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. Aa × Aa gives AA, Aa, Aa and aa in equal chances, so one in four children is aa.
15.A phenotype is influenced by:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Phenotype is the observable result of the genotype and the environment acting together.

Section B — True / False

20 marks

10 questions · 2 marks each.

16.All microorganisms cause disease.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Most microorganisms are harmless, and many are useful, for example gut bacteria and yeast.
17.Some bacteria are beneficial to humans and ecosystems.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Bacteria help with digestion, decomposition and nutrient cycling.
18.Viruses generally require host cells to reproduce.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Viruses have no cell machinery of their own and use a host cell to make new virus particles.
19.A pathogen must always cause symptoms immediately after entering a host.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is False. There is usually an incubation period, and some infections cause no symptoms.
20.Washing hands with soap can reduce the spread of some infections.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Washing removes many microbes from the skin and so reduces transmission by contact.
21.DNA contains information encoded in the sequence of its nucleotides.Intermediate2 pts
Why: The answer is True. The order of the bases A, T, C and G carries the genetic information.
22.Every mutation causes a visible disease.Intermediate2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Many mutations are neutral and have no visible effect.
23.Meiosis normally produces genetically identical daughter cells.Advanced2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Meiosis produces genetically different haploid cells. Mitosis produces identical cells.
24.A dominant allele is always more common in a population than a recessive allele.Advanced2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Dominance describes how an allele is expressed, not how common it is.
25.Genetic information can influence traits without being the only influence on every trait.Advanced2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Many traits are affected by both genes and the environment.

Section C — Fill in the Blank

20 marks

10 questions · 2 marks each. Type the missing word or term.

26.Viruses are generally described as ______ because they lack cellular organisation.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Acellular means not made of cells.
27.The spread of a pathogen from one host to another is called ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Transmission can be by contact, air, water, food or vectors.
28.An animal such as a mosquito that carries a pathogen from one host to another is called a ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: A vector transmits a pathogen without necessarily being ill itself.
29.Bacteria are ______ organisms because their cells have no nucleus.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Prokaryotes lack a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
30.The time between infection and the first symptoms is called the ______ period.Intermediate2 pts
Why: The incubation period varies between diseases.
31.A cell containing two sets of chromosomes is described as ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Most body cells are diploid. Gametes are haploid.
32.The process by which the information in a gene is used to make a functional product is gene ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Gene expression includes making RNA and, for many genes, protein.
33.The genetic make-up of an organism is its ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Genotype is the combination of alleles an organism has.
34.The observable characteristics of an organism form its ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Phenotype results from genotype and environment.
35.Exchange of DNA between homologous chromosomes during meiosis is called ______ over.Advanced2 pts
Why: Crossing over increases genetic variation in gametes.
