Section A — Multiple Choice

30 marks

15 questions · 2 marks each. Choose the best answer.

1.Which statement best explains why biology is considered an integrative science?Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. Biology links many levels of life, from molecules to ecosystems, and uses ideas from chemistry and physics. That is why it is called integrative.
2.Which structure is the basic unit of life?Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. The cell is the smallest unit that shows all the features of life. Tissues, organs and ecosystems are larger levels built from cells.
3.A cell membrane is especially important because it:Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. The membrane is selectively permeable: it lets some substances cross and keeps others out. Hereditary information is stored in DNA, not in the membrane.
4.Which sequence moves from smaller to larger levels of biological organisation?Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs work together in an organism. The other sequences run in the wrong order.
5.An ecosystem includes:Basic2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. An ecosystem is both the living part (biotic) and the non-living part (abiotic: water, soil, light, temperature), plus the interactions between them.
6.Which example is a population?Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. A population is one species in one area. All organisms in a pond make a community; a frog with its water is part of an ecosystem.
7.Biodiversity can include variation:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Biodiversity is measured at three levels: genetic diversity, species diversity and ecosystem diversity.
8.A hypothesis is strongest scientifically when it:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. A good hypothesis makes predictions that observations or experiments could support or contradict. Agreement or opinion alone does not make it scientific.
9.Homeostasis refers to:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. Homeostasis keeps conditions such as temperature and water balance within a range that cells can tolerate.
10.Natural selection can change a population when:Intermediate2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Selection needs variation that is inherited and that affects survival or reproduction. Individuals do not choose their mutations.
11.Which observation is quantitative?Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is C. A quantitative observation is a measurement with a number and a unit. The others describe qualities without measuring them.
12.A food web is more informative than a simple food chain because it:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. A food chain follows one path of energy. A food web joins many chains and shows that most organisms feed on, and are eaten by, several others.
13.If a habitat loses a major producer, the first major ecological effect is likely to involve:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is D. Producers bring energy into the food web. Consumers cannot make their own food, so less producer energy means less for every level above.
14.Which statement best connects evolution and biodiversity?Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is B. Over long periods, evolution produces new species and differences between groups. This is one major source of biodiversity.
15.A strong biological explanation of a declining frog population should first:Advanced2 pts
Why: The correct answer is A. Population declines usually have several interacting causes, so a good explanation starts from evidence over time and considers more than one factor.

Section B — True / False

20 marks

10 questions · 2 marks each.

16.All living organisms are multicellular.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Many organisms, such as bacteria and amoeba, are made of a single cell.
17.Cells maintain organised internal processes using energy and information.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Cells use energy (for example from food) and genetic information (DNA) to keep their processes organised.
18.A community includes organisms of different species living and interacting in an area.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is True. A community is all the populations of different species living together in one area.
19.An ecosystem contains only living components.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is False. An ecosystem also includes non-living parts such as water, soil, light and temperature.
20.Homeostasis means that every internal condition remains perfectly constant.Basic2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Conditions vary a little; homeostasis keeps them within a suitable range, not perfectly fixed.
21.A scientific conclusion can be revised when strong new evidence appears.Intermediate2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Science is self-correcting: conclusions change when better evidence is found.
22.Natural selection acts on populations across generations, not because individuals deliberately choose to evolve.Intermediate2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Selection is a process in populations over generations. Individuals do not choose it.
23.Biodiversity refers only to the number of species in a habitat.Advanced2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Biodiversity also includes genetic variation within species and variety of ecosystems.
24.Energy generally enters many ecosystems through producers and is transferred through feeding relationships.Advanced2 pts
Why: The answer is True. Producers capture energy (usually sunlight) and pass it on when consumers eat them.
25.A correlation by itself always proves that one biological variable causes another.Advanced2 pts
Why: The answer is False. Two variables can change together without one causing the other. Cause needs controlled evidence.

Section C — Fill in the Blank

20 marks

10 questions · 2 marks each. Type the missing word or term.

26.The basic structural and functional unit of life is the ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: The cell is the basic unit of life.
27.The maintenance of relatively stable internal conditions is called ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Homeostasis keeps internal conditions within a suitable range.
28.A group of organisms of the same species living in an area is a ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: One species in one area is a population.
29.The variety of life at genetic, species and ecosystem levels is called ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Biodiversity covers genes, species and ecosystems.
30.Organisms that make organic food from inorganic sources are called ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Producers (autotrophs) build food from simple inorganic substances, usually using light energy.
31.A proposed explanation that can be tested is a ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: A hypothesis is a testable proposed explanation.
32.The process by which populations change across generations is called ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Evolution is change in the inherited features of populations over generations.
33.The biological study of interactions between organisms and their environment is called ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Ecology studies how organisms interact with each other and their environment.
34.A group of different tissues working together to perform a specific function forms an ______.Intermediate2 pts
Why: Tissues combine to form organs, for example the heart or a leaf.
35.A network of interconnected food chains is a ______.Advanced2 pts
Why: Joined food chains make a food web.
