Punctuation Practice

Tap the gap where punctuation belongs, then choose the correct mark. Twelve sentences covering commas, apostrophes, and more.

Punctuation is invisible in speech but essential on the page — a single missing comma can genuinely change what a sentence means. This activity trains you to spot exactly where it belongs.

How to Use This Activity

1. Each sentence has a few dotted gaps — one of them needs a punctuation mark.
2. Tap the gap you think needs punctuation.
3. Choose the correct mark from the options shown.
4. You'll see instant feedback and a short rule explanation.
5. Complete all 12 sentences, then try the quiz below.
