EQ Test: Empathy and Relationships

24 everyday situations on listening, teamwork and kindness. Choose the best response and see why.

Empathy is not only feeling for someone. It is noticing what they need and responding well. This auto-graded test gives you 24 everyday situations about listening, inclusion, culture, teamwork and conflict. See the best answer straight away and finish with a score and a next step.

Key Takeaways

Empathy has two parts: understanding what someone feels, and caring enough to respond well. People usually need to feel heard before they can use advice, and listening works best when you ask rather than assume, especially across cultures and abilities. Teamwork improves when conflict is about the problem and shared goals, not about blame.

1.How This Test Works

Part 1 covers listening and understanding, Part 2 covers inclusion and respect for differences, and Part 3 covers teamwork and conflict. The best answers acknowledge feelings, ask instead of assuming, and keep the focus on the problem. The tool explains every answer and links to a related article. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and the answers reshuffle each time you start.

3.Using Your Result

Choose one practice for this week, such as asking "Do you want advice or just someone to listen?", putting your phone away when someone talks to you, or inviting one quiet person into a conversation. Real situations vary with the people and culture involved. If a situation involves bullying, abuse or a threat to safety, speak to a trusted adult.
