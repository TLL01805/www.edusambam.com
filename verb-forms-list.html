The Complete Verb Forms List: 1,000+ Verbs

All six forms of more than a thousand verbs in one searchable A to Z table: infinitive, base, S/ES, past, past participle and -ing.

EDUSAMBAM Editorial Team | 10 min read | Last updated: November 2026 | Grammar

Every verb has six forms. This A to Z list gives all six for 1,045 common English verbs, regular and irregular, so you can check any form in seconds.

Use it together with Verb Conjugation: The Six Forms of a Verb, which explains what each form does and how the spelling rules work. Spelling follows British English.

1.How to Read the Table

  • Infinitive is to plus the base form. It is never the main verb of a sentence.
  • Base form (V1) is used with I, you, we, they and plural nouns, and after do, does, did and modal verbs.
  • S / ES form is used only in the present simple with he, she, it or a singular noun.
  • Past simple (V2) is the second form. Past participle (V3) is the third form and needs a helper such as has, had or was.
  • Present participle (V4) is the -ing form and needs a helper such as is or was.
Regular and irregular

This list has 1,045 rows: 801 regular and 244 irregular. For regular verbs, V2 and V3 are the same and end in -ed. Irregular verbs are shown with their changed forms in red.

  • Lie appears twice: to lie down (lay, lain) and to tell an untruth (lied).
  • Hang is shown as hung. When a person is executed, the forms are hanged.
  • Get has the past participle got in British English and gotten in American English.
  • Be has the present forms am, is, are and the past forms was, were.
  • Modal verbs (can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would, ought to) are not listed, because they have no S/ES, participle or infinitive forms.
