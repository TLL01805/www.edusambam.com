Listening Practice & Comprehension: Guided Tours

Format: IELTS Section 2 — a single-speaker tour or orientation talk, with an interactive floor-plan to label

Your device reads a short orientation talk or guided tour aloud — a community centre, a library, a museum. As the speaker describes the layout, tap each numbered room on the plan and choose what it is from the word bank, then answer two quick detail questions. Switch to Exam Mode when you're ready for a single, real-pace play.

How this helps your listening

  1. Choose a tour below — each is a single speaker describing a place, just like IELTS Section 2.
  2. Pick Practice Mode (unlimited replays, adjustable speed) while you're learning, or Exam Mode (one play only, real pace) to test yourself properly.
  3. As you listen, tap a numbered room on the plan, then tap its name in the word bank below to label it. Answer the two detail questions too.
