Computer Terms Crossword

Identify ten basic computer terms from their definitions, then complete the crossword and check your answers.

EDUSAMBAM Interactive Activity•Computing Foundations

A computer has many parts, programs and services. Learning the basic vocabulary makes it easier to understand how computers work and how people use them. In this activity, each answer is a computer term described by a definition. Use the clues to fill the crossword.

1.How to Play

Read the definitions in the Across and Down sections. Click a numbered square or clue, type your answer, and move through the crossword. Answers use letters only; spaces are not needed.

Learning tip

Do not look for the answer first. Think about the function described by the definition. For example, a device used to display text and images is a monitor, while a program that helps you access websites is a browser.

2.Computer Terms Crossword

Complete all 10 words from their definitions. The crossword is designed to help you connect each term with what it does.

This activity introduces common computer vocabulary through definitions rather than simple memorisation. You are practising the connection between a term and its function.
