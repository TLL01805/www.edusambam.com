How this helps your speaking

  1. Choose a topic below — each one has four short questions, just like a real Part 1 interview.
  2. Pick Practice Mode (tips visible, up to 90 seconds to answer) while you're learning the format, or Exam Mode (tips hidden, strict 30-second limit per answer) to test yourself properly.
  3. Tap the microphone to record your answer, then play it back and listen honestly, the way an examiner would hear it.
  4. Reveal the model answer, then work through the band-descriptor checklist after all four questions in the topic.

Tips for Part 1 Answers

  • Keep answers to about 2–4 sentences — not a single word, but not a two-minute speech either
  • Answer the question directly first, then add a reason, example, or extra detail
  • Use natural spoken language and contractions (I'm, don't, it's) rather than overly formal phrasing
  • Don't worry about small grammar slips — focus on speaking clearly and naturally
  • Avoid memorised answers; examiners can usually tell, and it can affect your fluency score if it sounds rehearsed

Self-Assessment for This Topic

Fluency & Coherence
  • I spoke at a natural pace, without long pauses or excessive hesitation
  • My answers were connected and easy to follow
  • I extended each answer with a reason or example, not just one-word replies
Lexical Resource
  • I used a range of vocabulary rather than repeating the same words
  • I used topic-appropriate words naturally, without translating word-for-word in my head
Grammatical Range & Accuracy
  • I used a mix of simple and slightly more complex sentences
  • I used tenses correctly for what I was describing
Pronunciation
  • Listening back, my speech was clear and easy to understand
  • I used natural stress and intonation, rather than a flat tone
