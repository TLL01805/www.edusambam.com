Reading Practice & Comprehension: Auto-Scroll

Technique: Auto-Scroll — the passage glides upward on its own, and each line fades away as it nears the top

The whole article scrolls upward at a steady pace, like a teleprompter — you don't control it. As each line nears the top of the panel it fades away for good, so you have to read it before it's gone. There's no scrolling back and no pausing to reread — just the steady forward motion that strong, confident readers build over time.
