How Is Water Formed?

Water is a compound made from two elements, hydrogen and oxygen. One water molecule has 2 hydrogen atoms joined to 1 oxygen atom, so its formula is H₂O.

The atoms are held together by covalent bonds, where atoms share electrons. Water forms when hydrogen gas burns in oxygen and releases heat. Chemists write the reaction like this:

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
