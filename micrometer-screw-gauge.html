How to Use a Micrometer Screw Gauge

A micrometer screw gauge measures very small lengths, such as the diameter of a wire or the thickness of paper, correct to 0.01 mm. It works on the screw principle: each full turn of the thimble moves the spindle forward by a fixed distance called the pitch.

Least count = pitch ÷ number of divisions
Observed reading = MSR + (CSR × least count)
Correct reading = observed reading − zero error

This activity uses a screw gauge with a pitch of 1 mm and 100 divisions on the circular scale. MSR means main scale reading and CSR means circular scale reading.

  1. Learn the parts of the screw gauge.
  2. Find the least count.
  3. Check the zero error.
  4. Close the jaws on a wire.
  5. Read the scales and correct the result.
