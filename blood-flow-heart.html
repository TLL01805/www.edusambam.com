Blood Flow Through the Heart

The heart is a muscular pump with four chambers. Humans have a double circulation: blood passes through the heart twice in every complete trip around the body. The right side pumps blood to the lungs, and the left side pumps it to the body.

Body → right side → lungs → left side → body
