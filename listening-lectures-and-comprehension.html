Listening Practice & Comprehension: Academic Lectures

Format: IELTS Section 4 — a single, continuous academic lecture with no breaks, followed by summary completion

This is the hardest of the four IELTS Listening sections, and this tool is built to match it exactly: one speaker, one long uninterrupted lecture, denser academic content than anywhere else on the site, and — as in the real test — you complete a summary using no more than two words and/or a number for each answer.

How this helps your listening

  1. Choose a lecture below — each is a single, continuous academic talk, just like IELTS Section 4. There's no dialogue to re-anchor your attention, so concentration matters more here than anywhere else.
  2. Pick Practice Mode (unlimited replays, adjustable speed) while you're learning, or Exam Mode (one play only, real pace) to test yourself the way the real section works — Section 4 is only ever played once, even in the real exam.
  3. Complete the summary as you listen. Write no more than two words and/or a number for each answer, exactly as the real test requires.
  4. Submit to see your score, then reveal the full transcript to see exactly what you missed and why.
