Word order is one of the fastest ways to spot a grammar mistake, and one of the hardest instincts to build just from reading rules. This activity trains that instinct directly.

How to Use This Activity

1. Each round shows a set of scrambled words below the answer box.
2. Tap words in order to build the sentence in the box above — tap a placed word again to remove it.
3. Tap "Check Answer" once every word is placed.
4. Use "Reset" to clear your current attempt, or "Skip" to move on.
5. Finish all 12 sentences to see your final score, then try the quiz below.
