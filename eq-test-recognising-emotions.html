EQ Test: Recognising Emotions

Emotional intelligence starts with noticing. This test gives you 24 situations about reading faces, voices, messages and your own body signals. It is auto-graded: tap an answer and you see the best choice straight away, with a reason, and a score at the end.

Key Takeaways

Perceiving emotions is the first branch of the ability model of emotional intelligence. Faces, voices, posture and context are clues rather than proof, and people often guess wrongly, especially in text messages. The most accurate readers combine several cues and then check their guess by asking, gently, instead of deciding on the spot.

1.How This Test Works

Part 1 is about faces, voices and behaviour. Part 2 covers messages and the context around them. Part 3 is about reading your own body signals, mixed feelings and patterns. Tap the answer you think is best. The tool marks it at once, explains the reasoning and links to a related article. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and the answers reshuffle each time you start.

3.Using Your Result

Treat your result as practice, not a verdict. Try one habit this week: when you notice a strong cue in someone, offer a gentle guess such as "You seem tired, is that right?" instead of deciding what they feel. Notice your own body signals and give them a word. Real people are more varied than any test, and cues differ between cultures.
