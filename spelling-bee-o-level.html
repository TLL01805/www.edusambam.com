Spelling Bee Test: O Level (Grades 8 to 10)

Hear each word, spell it with the keys or your voice, and see your score at once. 80 words, auto-checked.

EDUSAMBAM Editorial Team | 80 words | Last updated: November 2026 | Spelling

This test is for O Level students in Grades 8 to 10. It has 80 words of standard academic vocabulary.

Listen to each word, spell it, and get your score at once. Your result shows every word with your spelling next to the correct spelling, so you know exactly what to practise.

1.How This Test Works

  • Listen. Tap the round speaker button to hear the word. The word is never shown on the screen.
  • Use hints. Tap Definition, Sentence or Origin to hear more, as judges give in a real spelling bee. Hints are spoken only.
  • Spell. Use the letter keys on the screen, or tap the microphone and say the letters one by one. The choice is yours.
  • Check. Tap the check button to go to the next word. Your answers are marked automatically at the end.

2.Take the Test

There are 80 words in this test, and the order changes each time. Turn up your volume before you begin.

Your browser cannot read words aloud. Please open this page in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

3.Practice Tips

  • Learn words in families. Knowing that benevolent comes from Latin roots meaning "well" and "wish" helps with related words.
  • Write every missed word three times, then say its letters aloud without looking.
  • Retake the test later. The words come in a new order each time.

The microphone option needs an internet connection and works best in Chrome on Android phones and computers. If it does not work on your device, use the letter keys.
