EQ Test: Communication and Assertiveness

24 situations on speaking up kindly, listening well, and giving and receiving feedback. Choose the best response and learn why.

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Written by the EDUSAMBAM Editorial Team. This tool is for general education and practice and does not replace advice from a qualified professional.

Good communication turns feelings into clear, kind words and turns listening into understanding. This auto-graded test gives you 24 situations about speaking up, listening and feedback. Tap the best response, read the reason at once, and finish with a score and a skill to practise.

Key Takeaways

Assertive communication sits between staying silent and attacking: you state what you think, feel or need, clearly and respectfully. Listening well means summarising what you heard and asking open questions. Feedback is easiest to use when it is specific, kind and private, and when the person receiving it separates the tone from the useful content.

1.How This Test Works

Part 1 covers speaking up assertively, Part 2 covers active listening and clear questions, and Part 3 covers giving and receiving feedback. The best answers are calm, specific and respectful. They use "I" statements, open questions and clear requests, and avoid silence, blame and sarcasm. The tool explains every answer and links to a related article. Nothing is saved, and the answers reshuffle each time.

3.Using Your Result

Pick one skill to practise this week, such as saying a clear, kind no, paraphrasing what a friend says before you reply, or giving one specific compliment and one specific suggestion. Real conversations are messier than a test and depend on the people and culture involved. If a situation involves bullying, abuse or a threat to safety, speak to a trusted adult.

Sources and Evidence
