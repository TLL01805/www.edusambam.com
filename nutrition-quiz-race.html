Human Nutrition Quiz Race

Answer timed nutrition questions and earn speed points. Choose Basic, Intermediate or Advanced.

EDUSAMBAM Editorial Team|5 min play|Human Nutrition · Basic, Intermediate, Advanced

Can you answer quickly and correctly? Choose a level, then race the timer. After each answer you see a short explanation, so you learn even when you miss.

Choose your level

Question 1 / 8Score 0Time left 15s

How the Points Work

A correct answer gives 10 points plus 1 point for every second left on the timer. Basic gives 20 seconds, Intermediate 15 and Advanced 12. A wrong answer or a timeout scores 0. You can press the keys 1 to 4 to answer and Enter for the next question.

Tips for a High Score

  • Read the whole question first, then check all four choices.
  • Remove answers you know are wrong, then choose from the rest.
  • Read each explanation. It teaches the rule behind the answer.

This activity is for general education only. It is not medical advice. Please ask a doctor or registered dietitian about personal health or diet questions.
