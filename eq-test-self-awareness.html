Self-awareness is the starting point of emotional intelligence: noticing what you feel, what you are good at, where you struggle, and what you say to yourself. This auto-graded test gives you 24 situations in three parts. Tap an answer, see the best choice at once with a reason, and finish with a score and a next step.

Key Takeaways

Researchers separate two kinds of self-awareness: how clearly you see yourself, and how well you understand how others see you. The two do not always match, which is why feedback matters. How you talk to yourself matters too. Studies suggest that addressing yourself by name, as you would a friend, can lower stress, and that self-compassion supports healthier coping than harsh self-criticism.

1.How This Test Works

Part 1 is about reading your own feelings and triggers. Part 2 covers strengths, limits and blind spots. Part 3 looks at the voice in your head, including common thinking traps such as all-or-nothing thoughts and mind-reading. Tap the answer you think is best. The tool marks it at once, explains the reasoning and links to a related article. Nothing you choose is saved or sent anywhere, and the answer order changes each time you start.

3.Using Your Result

Treat your result as a snapshot, not a label. Choose one habit from the explanations, such as naming a feeling in one word at the end of each day, asking one trusted person for specific feedback, or answering a harsh thought the way you would answer a friend. If low or anxious feelings stay heavy for weeks, or you are worried about your safety, please talk to a trusted adult, doctor or counsellor.
