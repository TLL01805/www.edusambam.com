      How to Play

      Tap a square or a clue, then type your answer. The cursor moves on by itself. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down. “Check answers” shows right letters in green and wrong letters in red. “Reveal letter” fills in one letter for the selected word.

      Why Solve Crosswords?

      A crossword makes you think about meaning and spelling together. The crossing letters give you hints, so each correct answer helps you find the next one.

      This activity is for general education only. It is not medical advice. Please ask a doctor or registered dietitian about personal health or diet questions.
